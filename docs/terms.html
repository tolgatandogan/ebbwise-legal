2. what ebbwise is — and isn't

Ebbwise helps people living with multiple sclerosis plan around their energy levels, using their own check-in history and weather data for their location.

Ebbwise is not a medical device and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment. Nothing it shows you — a day's band, a recommendation sentence, a pattern card — is a prediction of your health or a substitute for a doctor, neurologist, or other qualified professional. Everything is described in plain, past-tense language, built from your own records. Always consult a qualified health professional for decisions about your health.

3. accounts

Ebbwise works fully without an account — nothing in the app is gated behind signing in. If you choose to sign in with Apple, it is used only to let you manage or delete an account-linked identity from another device; it does not sync your health data, which stays on your device either way. You can delete your account at any time from the Profile screen.

4. subscriptions and purchases

Ebbwise Plus is offered as auto-renewing subscriptions, purchased through the Apple App Store:

  • Monthly — billed every month.
  • Yearly — includes a 7-day free trial, then billed annually.

Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. Subscriptions automatically renew unless auto-renew is turned off at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Your account will be charged for renewal within 24 hours prior to the end of the current period, at the same price you were originally charged unless the price has changed. You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your device's Apple ID account settings; the free trial (if applicable) is forfeited if you purchase a subscription during the trial period.

Ebbwise also offers a one-time, non-renewing gift purchase — a year of Ebbwise Plus for someone else. A gift purchase does not renew, and does not unlock Ebbwise Plus for the purchaser's own account.

Refund requests are handled by Apple under the App Store's standard refund policies; Ebbwise does not process refunds directly.
